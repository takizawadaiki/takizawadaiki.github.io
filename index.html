02 / Method, live

A SIMULATION YOU CAN RUN
An example you can run

If Team A wins 55% of its games, how often does it win a best-of-7 series?

There are two ways to find out: work it out with probability, or play the series thousands of times and count. This does both, so you can watch the count land on the exact answer.

Why it matters. Real matchups have injuries, home advantage and form, and no formula covers all of that. Counting simulated games still works, which is how my six prediction models produce their odds. This example keeps every game at the same probability so that an exact answer exists to check against.
